Apigee X + Gemini Token Quota Architecture

Declarative Quota & AI Governance
STEP 1
4 Client Applications

Basic Tier: Smart TV & Audio Signal (4k limit). Premium Tier: Core & Wireless (40k limit). Authenticates via x-api-key.

STEP 2
VerifyAPIKey Policy

Validates client key against API Product, resolves tier limits, and dynamically extracts Department & Sub Department metadata.

STEP 3
LLMTokenQuota Policy

Enforces 5-minute rolling window quota in PreFlow. Burst overruns immediately trigger HTTP 429 (RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED).

STEP 4
Vertex AI Gemini

Apigee mints OAuth GoogleAccessToken (roles/aiplatform.user) and calls gemini-3.5-flash-lite via the global endpoint.

STEP 5
Debit & Telemetry

LLMTokenQuota-Count debits response tokens. JS-ExtractAiTokens & DC-AiTelemetry stream 6 dimensions to Apigee Analytics.
